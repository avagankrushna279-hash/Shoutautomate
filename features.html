Frequently asked questions

Explore answers about Shout Automate

What is Shout Automate used for?

Shout Automate connects CRM, funnels, forms, workflows, WhatsApp, calendar, payments and AI in one place, so every enquiry is captured and followed up.

Can I see a demo before I buy?

Yes. Book a demo and we’ll show you the platform for your industry.

How can AI handle my follow-ups?

AI Brain holds your business knowledge and AI Agents handle repetitive conversations and replies.

Can I customise workflows for my industry?

Yes. Workflows and CRM pipeline stages are set up around your industry’s own journey.

Which industries do you support?

We have solutions for 19 industries, from real estate and education to insurance, hotels and events.
