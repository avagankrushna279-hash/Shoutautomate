Frequently asked questions

What is Shout Automate used for?

Shout Automate is an AI business automation platform that brings CRM, websites and funnels, forms, workflows, WhatsApp, calendar, payments and AI into one system, so every enquiry is captured, followed up and converted.

Can I see a demo before I buy?

Yes. Book a demo and we’ll walk you through the platform for your industry, from the first enquiry to the repeat customer.

How can AI handle my follow-ups?

AI Brain holds your business knowledge, and AI Agents handle repetitive conversations and replies, so leads get answered quickly while your team focuses on the conversations that need a person.

Can I customise workflows for my industry?

Yes. Workflows automate follow-up and lifecycle actions, and each solution is built around your industry’s own journey and CRM pipeline stages.

Which industries do you support?

We have solutions for 19 industries, including real estate, training and coaching, education, health and wellness, insurance, hotels, travel, events, beauty and salon, and more.

What does Shout Automate cost?

Websites start at ₹4,999. AI Software Only is ₹7,999/month, Lead Automation is ₹9,999/month and the Full Business Suite is ₹19,999/month. Quarterly and annual pricing is available.

Can agencies white-label it?

Yes. Agencies can use their own logo, colours, domain and branded AI assistant, and manage separate client sub-accounts from one dashboard.
